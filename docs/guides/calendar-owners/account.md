# Manage your account

> Status: placeholder. This guide may be written after launch if it's needed; the account UI is mostly self-explanatory.

Account-level settings — the things that are about *you* rather than about a particular calendar. Password and email.

## Planned scope

- Changing your password
- Changing your email address
- Account-level settings

For now, these settings live under **Settings → Account**: **Profile** (display name and language; your username is shown there but cannot be changed) and **Security** (email, password). **Log out** is the last row of the **Settings** list.
